# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 26 July 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to "Assistant for Fate" ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

## 2. Data Collection

The App does not collect any personal data from users. We do not request, store, or transmit any personal information such as names, email addresses, or contact details. Your characters and settings never leave your device.

The App does connect to the internet for one purpose only: downloading optional community mods and their catalog, as described in Section 5. These requests never include your character data, your settings, or any personal information.

## 3. Data Storage

All data related to your characters and preferences (e.g., theme, language, dice roll settings, installed mods) is stored locally on your device using internal browser storage mechanisms like local storage and IndexedDB. This data remains on your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

## 4. User Control

You have full control over your data within the App. You can update or delete your characters, preferences, and installed mods through the App's user interface. Additionally, you can clear all App data via your device's settings.

## 5. Network Connections and Third-Party Content

The App supports optional, community-created mods. To make them available, the App performs the following network requests:

- **Mod catalog refresh (automatic):** when the App starts, it downloads the public mod catalog from the official mod registry, hosted on GitHub Pages (`stanislavsonder.github.io/fate-core-mods`). This keeps the Mod Store listing up to date.
- **Mod Store browsing (on your action):** opening a mod's page in the Mod Store downloads that mod's description from the same registry.
- **Mod installation (on your action):** installing a mod — from the Mod Store or from a URL you provide yourself — downloads the mod's files (its manifest, code bundle, and translations) from the registry or from that URL.

These are ordinary download requests. They carry no character data, no settings, and no identifiers added by the App. As with any internet connection, the hosting provider (GitHub, or the host of a URL you provide) can technically see standard connection metadata such as your IP address; this is governed by that provider's own privacy policy, not by us.

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. No data is shared with or collected by third parties by us.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages and does not contain content that requires age restrictions.

## 7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to you through a dialogue prompt upon launching the App after the policy is updated.
